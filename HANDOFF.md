## Done (on `main`)
Everything up to the Slang quiz is pushed to GitHub (26 October 2026). Ask the user before each push; they run it from their Terminal panel.

- **App shell:** React + Vite, dark green theme (colours are variables at the top of `src/styles.css`), sidebar that becomes a bottom bar on phones, dashboard with phrase of the day, streak, progress panel, backup/restore of progress as a JSON file. No leaderboard (user doesn't want one).
- **Speech out:** `src/lib/speech.js` only speaks with a Portuguese voice (prefers pt-PT), never an English one. `speak(text, { rate })` supports a slower rate.
- **Vocab (complete for Level 1):**
  - Content: `src/data/levels.json` (levels, units, `passMark` 0.8) and `src/data/vocab/level-1.json` (55 items in 5 topic units, words and phrases, all `verified: false`). Nouns are stored without the article plus `gender`; `displayPt()` adds o/a. `alternatives` lists other accepted answers; `blank` is the gap word in the example sentence.
  - Casual pt-PT is the default (*desculpa*, *como estás?*); the polite form is in `alternatives` or the note. Polite forms stay where they're natural (waiter, strangers).
  - Each unit: Flashcards (spaced repetition, `src/lib/srs.js`), Multiple choice (Portuguese to English, English to Portuguese, fill the gap), Type it (from English). Wrong answers return at the end of a session; green tick / red cross feedback.
  - Marking (`src/lib/answers.js`): ignores capitals, punctuation and missing accents (shows the right spelling), nouns with or without article; anything else only if listed in `alternatives`. Never guesses.
  - **Level 2** ("Everyday life", added 27 October 2026): `src/data/vocab/level-2.json`, 54 items in 5 units (restaurant, shopping, asking the way, home and routine, days and plans), more phrases than Level 1. All `verified: false`.
  - **Levels 3–6** (added 28 October 2026, one file each in `src/data/vocab/`): 3 "Getting by" (work, health, weather, clothes, describing people), 4 "Out and about" (travel, hotel, food shopping, free time, feelings), 5 "Getting things done" (phone, bank and post office, renting and repairs, appointments, complaints), 6 "Opinions and the news" (opinions, agreeing, telling a story, news, nature). 221 items, all `verified: false`. Plural-only nouns (calças, férias, correios…) have no `gender`, so no wrong o/a is added. Every item was typed through in the browser and marked right.
  - **Levels 7–9** (added 27 October 2026, chosen by how common the words are, not by topic): 7 "Core verbs" (ser, estar, ter, ir, saber, querer, sair, pedir, comer…), 8 "Describing and linking" (bom/mau, fácil, cheio, pronto, sempre/nunca/ainda/já, mas/porque/enquanto/sem…), 9 "Everyday essentials" (coisa, pessoa, cidade, sítio, cedo/tarde, mês/ano, trabalhar, morar, conseguir, quem/onde/qual, nada/ninguém/alguém…). 60 items each, 12 per unit, including a few 3+ word phrases so each level has a word-order round. Verbs are stored as the infinitive (reflexives as *lembrar-se*, with the plain verb accepted); adjectives list the feminine in `alternatives`. All typed through in the browser and marked right; word order played through. All `verified: false`. **Vocab now has 510 items** towards the agreed 1,000 (B1 is roughly 2,000+, say so if asked).
  - **Word order** (per level, `src/pages/vocab/WordOrderSession.jsx`): tap word tiles to build every phrase of 3+ words in the level. Only fixed phrases are used, so one order is right; any other natural order must be listed in `alternatives` (done for the "por favor" / "obrigado" / time-first variants). Best score saved per level in `vocab.order`. It doesn't touch spaced repetition. Also used in the middle part of the level test.
  - Level test: 20 questions getting harder (choice, typed gap, typing), marked at the end (silent on purpose, user agreed). 80% passes and opens the next level permanently. Retake any time. From Level 2 on, a quarter of questions come from earlier levels.
- **Reading:** `src/data/reading/level-1.json`, 3 texts (café, family, station). Every word has a meaning in the text's `glossary` (tap to see it). Listen to the text, 3 comprehension questions, best score saved. All `verified: false`.
- **Speaking (built, switched off):** listen, speak into the mic, see which words the browser's recogniser picked up (`src/lib/recognition.js`). Honest labels: transcription only, not a pronunciation score; audio goes to Google/Microsoft in Chrome/Edge. It could not be tried with a real mic (the agent's preview pane blocks microphones), so the user asked to lock it. Turn it on with `speakingReady` in `src/App.jsx` and `ready` for Speaking in `src/pages/Dashboard.jsx`.
- **Grammar:** grouped into stages (`src/lib/grammar.js` lists one JSON file per stage; topics are numbered straight through). Stage "Building blocks" (`basics.json`, 4 topics: gender and plurals, contractions do/na/ao/pelo, possessives, este/esse/aquele) uses `"focus": "word"` so practice says "missing word" and each drill gives its own `hint`; tables can set `listen` ("form" or "both") and `caption`. Later stages, in order: "Have done, had done" (`perfect.json`: tenho feito, tinha feito), "Subjunctive" (`subjunctive.json`: present forms, when to use, imperfect, future, choosing), "Personal infinitive" (`infinitive.json`), "Linking words" (`linking.json`: por/para, que/quem/onde/o que, porque/por isso/embora). Stage "Future and \"would\"" (`future.json`, 4 topics: future tense, conditional, será que / guessing, mesoclisis for recognition). Stage "Commands" (`commands.json`, 3 topics: tu do/don't, polite and vocês, commands with pronouns). Stage "Pronouns" (`pronouns.json`, 4 topics: after the verb, in front after não/question words/que/já, lhe/lhes, lo/la/no/na) adds an `order` drill: word tiles from `answer` (hyphenated pronouns become their own tile, first word lowercased), marked ignoring hyphens, other natural orders in `alternatives`. Stage "Past tense" (`past.json`, 5 perfeito topics: -ar, -er/-ir, fui/tive/estive, fiz/disse/vi/dei, vim/pus/pude/quis; then `imperfect.json`, 4 imperfeito topics: regular, era/tinha/estava/ia, perfeito vs imperfeito, estava a + polite queria/podia/gostava) adds a `rewrite` drill: shows `from` (a present sentence, labelled "Now:") and asks for the past form in the gap. Stage "Everyday patterns" (`patterns.json`, 4 topics: reflexive verbs, estar a + verb, verb + verb patterns such as tenho de / costumo / preciso de, ficar). Stage "Present tense" (`present.json`), 15 topics: ser, estar, ser or estar?, ter, ir, regular -ar/-er/-ir, fazer, poder, querer, then irregular groups (ver/dar/ler, dizer/trazer/saber, vir/pôr, pedir/ouvir/dormir/sair). Topics with several verbs use `tables` (one per verb, with meaning) instead of `table`; each drill names its verb with a `verb` field and can list accepted spellings in `alternatives` (e.g. oiço, lêem). Each topic: explanation, verb table with Listen, examples, pt-PT notes, then practice (type the form, type the verb in a sentence, pick ser/estar with a reason). Every drill is written by hand in the data; nothing is generated. No vós; você included with a note on how it sounds in Portugal. All `verified: false`.
- **Menu:** 6 items (Home, Vocab, Speaking, Reading, Grammar, Slang); the user chose to keep Speaking visible while it's switched off.
- **Slang:** `src/data/slang/pt-pt.json`, 29 expressions in groups (everyday chat, describing, people, café and bar with Lisbon/Porto words, exclamations, Portugal vs Brazil false friends, swear words). Each has formality and region tags, an example with Listen, and `confidence`; medium confidence shows a "Not sure: ask your native speaker" tag. Swear words are hidden until "Show swear words" is switched on, and are never in the quiz. Quiz: 10 random "what does it mean?" questions; wrong options never share a main word with the right one. All `verified: false`.

- **Weak / Secure / Mastered** (user request 27 October 2026: "you can't have learnt everything in one go"). Rules in `src/lib/mastery.js`, shown with `src/components/Stages.jsx` (stage bar for vocab, coloured tag elsewhere; colours `--weak/--secure/--mastered`).
  - Vocab words: from the flashcard interval. Under 7 days = weak, 7+ = secure, 21+ = mastered. Typing right in one session only makes a word weak; it takes real days to move up. "I already know this" now makes a word secure (7 days), not mastered.
  - Grammar topics, reading texts, slang quiz: records keep `last`, `lastDate` and `passes` (days scored 80%+). Last score under 80% = weak; 80%+ = secure; 80%+ on 3 different days spanning a week = mastered. Old `{best, total}` records count their best as the last score.
  - Home "Carry on" suggests a weak grammar topic, or a secure one not practised for a week. Home areas show stage counts; "words mastered" replaced "words learned". Tile wall: a tile per vocab unit or grammar topic once it is secure.
